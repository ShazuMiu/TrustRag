The Padma Multipurpose Bridge is a road-rail bridge across the Padma River in Bangladesh. With a length of 6.15 kilometres it is the longest bridge in the country. It connects Mawa in Munshiganj District with Janjira in Shariatpur District, linking the south-western region of Bangladesh to the capital, Dhaka.

The bridge has two decks: a four-lane road on the upper deck and a single-track railway on the lower deck. The main bridge was built by China Major Bridge Engineering Company. The project was funded by the Government of Bangladesh from its own resources after the World Bank cancelled its planned loan in 2012. The bridge was opened to road traffic on 25 June 2022, and rail service across the bridge began in October 2023.
