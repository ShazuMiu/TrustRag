Kangchenjunga is the third-highest mountain in the world, with a height of 8,586 metres. It lies in the eastern Himalayas on the border between Nepal and the Indian state of Sikkim. The first ascent was made on 25 May 1955 by Joe Brown and George Band of a British expedition, who stopped just short of the true summit to respect the beliefs of the people of Sikkim.
