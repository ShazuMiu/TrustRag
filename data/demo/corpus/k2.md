K2 is the second-highest mountain on Earth, with a height of 8,611 metres. It lies in the Karakoram range on the border between Pakistan and China. K2 is known as the Savage Mountain because it is considered much harder and more dangerous to climb than Mount Everest.

The first ascent of K2 was made on 31 July 1954 by the Italian climbers Lino Lacedelli and Achille Compagnoni, members of an expedition led by Ardito Desio.
