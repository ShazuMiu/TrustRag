title: Major rivers of Bangladesh
source: Geography summary
year: 2020

Bangladesh is crossed by three great rivers: the Padma, the Jamuna and the Meghna. The Padma is the name of the main channel of the Ganges in Bangladesh. The Jamuna is the main distributary of the Brahmaputra River after it enters Bangladesh from India. The Jamuna joins the Padma near Goalundo, and the combined river later meets the Meghna near Chandpur before flowing into the Bay of Bengal. Together these rivers form the Ganges-Brahmaputra delta, the largest river delta in the world. Seasonal floods along these rivers bring fertile silt but also cause severe damage in many years.
