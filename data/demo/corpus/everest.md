Mount Everest is Earth's highest mountain above sea level. It is located in the Mahalangur Himal sub-range of the Himalayas, on the border between Nepal and the Tibet Autonomous Region of China. In December 2020, China and Nepal jointly announced its height as 8,848.86 metres.

The first confirmed ascent of Everest was made on 29 May 1953 by Edmund Hillary of New Zealand and Tenzing Norgay, a Sherpa from Nepal, as part of a British expedition. The two most common climbing routes are the south-east ridge from Nepal and the north ridge from Tibet.
