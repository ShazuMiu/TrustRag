How to read this

Faithfulness: share of answer sentences entailed by at least one retrieved passage (1 − hallucination rate). Citation recall: share of sentences whose cited passages entail them. Citation precision: share of citations that are actually needed (ALCE definition). Ghost citations: citations to sources that do not exist. If the judge NLI is the same model as the verifier, grounding metrics for TrustRAG are optimistic. Set evaluation.judge_nli_backend to a different model for an independent judge.
